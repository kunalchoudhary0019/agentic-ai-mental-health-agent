  <h1>🧠 Agentic AI Research & Nutrition Agent</h1>

  <h2>📌 Overview</h2>
  <p>
    The <strong>Agentic AI Research & Nutrition Agent</strong> is an IBM-powered application that integrates 
    <a href="ca://s?q=Explain_Agentic_AI">Agentic AI</a>, 
    <a href="ca://s?q=Explain_RAG_in_AI">Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)</a>, 
    and <a href="ca://s?q=Personalization_in_AI_nutrition_agents">personalization</a> into a unified intelligent system. 
    Unlike static AI tools, this solution is <strong>autonomous, adaptive, and proactive</strong>, capable of breaking down complex goals, retrieving real-time data, and orchestrating multi-agent workflows.
  </p>

  <h2>🚀 Features</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Multi_agent_intelligence_in_AI">Multi-Agent Intelligence</a> – Planner, Researcher, Critic, and Support agents collaborate for accuracy.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=RAG_accuracy_in_AI">RAG-Based Accuracy</a> – Combines retrieval with generation to reduce misinformation.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Hyper_personalization_in_AI">Hyper-Personalization</a> – Tailors diet and mental health insights to individual needs.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Real_time_feedback_in_AI">Real-Time Feedback Loop</a> – Continuously adapts based on food logs and user behavior.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Multi_modal_input_in_AI">Multi-Modal Input</a> – Supports text, voice, image, and video for richer analysis.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Preventive_healthcare_in_AI">Preventive Healthcare Focus</a> – Provides disease-specific nutrition and mental health advice.</li>
    <li><a href="ca://s?q=Scalable_AI_with_IBM_watsonx">Scalable & Enterprise-Ready</a> – Built on IBM watsonx.ai with Granite models, ensuring governance and compliance.</li>
  </ul>
